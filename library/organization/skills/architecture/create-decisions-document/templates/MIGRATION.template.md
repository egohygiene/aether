## Preview and preservation

Record the explicit authorization and every changed record, including inline records. Inventory existing IDs, prefixes, status claims, bodies, aliases and lineage. Compare numerical identities across widths before any rename.
Freeze collisions for human resolution. Preserve source history and blame links;
never invent rationale or grant an exception through this map.

## Validation and handoff

Record the exact policy/validator pins, commands, outcomes, missing evidence,
proposed records and human-owned disposition work. Cite the governing/new ADR or
explain why none is required.

## Rollback

Record the reviewed prior files/pins and applicable materializer state. Undo only
the reviewed migration/projection change after checking for later local edits;
retain authored ADR history and evidence. Do not erase subsequent decisions.
